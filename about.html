Free in your browser · desktop and phone · works offline

What you do

Throw

Flick to throw. Faster flicks throw harder. Topple the gems, score the gold, clear the board.

Blast

Bombs, chemicals and fireworks set each other off. Long chains earn bonus points and slow time down.

Pull

Grab a block and ease it out of the tower without bringing the whole thing down.

16 kinds of block

Wood, steel, ice, gems, clouds that float away, fragile blocks that shatter, blocks that play a tune.

Party on one screen

Two to four players take turns: a score duel, or a Jenga-off where the last one standing wins.

Lasers and water

Zap single blocks with a laser, or hose a wall until it gives way.

Controls

Mouse or touch: flick to throw · right-drag or two fingers to look around · pinch or wheel to zoom
Laptop touchpad: two-finger swipe to look around · pinch to zoom
Keyboard: arrows aim · hold Space to charge, let go to throw · Q/E turn · Z/X zoom · R restart
